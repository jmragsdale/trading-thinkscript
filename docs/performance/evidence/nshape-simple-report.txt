## Rules Tested

The detector mirrors the legacy ThinkScript 3-bar N pattern:

- C1: directional large-body candle with volume above the 20-bar average
- C2: pullback that does not break C1
- C3: breakout candle with volume above the 20-bar average
- Entry: next bar open after C3 confirmation
- Stop: C2 low for longs, C2 high for shorts
- Targets: `0.5R`, `1R`, `1.5R`, `2R`
- Max hold: `3`, `5`, `10`, `15` two-minute bars
- Session filter: RTH only
- Entry window: `09:35` to before `15:30`
- Ambiguous bars: stop first

## Verdict

Do not use this old 3-bar N pattern by itself as a SPY 2-minute scalping signal.

It can be kept as a candidate trigger, but it needs a filter before it is worth testing with options. The next useful tests are:

1. split by trend regime, such as above/below VWAP or 20 EMA;
2. split by time of day;
3. require range expansion or relative volume on C3;
4. test QQQ separately;
5. only then translate surviving timestamps into an options model.
