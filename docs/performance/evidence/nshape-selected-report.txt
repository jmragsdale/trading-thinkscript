# SPY N-Shape Quant Adjustment Report

Generated: 2026-07-16

## Objective

Find practical code or timeframe adjustments that can lift the legacy 3-bar N-shape pattern toward a `1.3` profit factor on SPY.

The original 2-minute standalone pattern was negative expectancy. The adjustment search tested:

- timeframe: `1m`, `2m`, `3m`, `5m`, `10m`, `15m`
- side: long, short, both
- time windows: full RTH, morning-only, afternoon-only
- filters: EMA trend, VWAP trend, C3 range expansion
- exits: `0.5R`, `1R`, `1.5R`, `2R`, `3R`
- max hold: `3`, `5`, `10`, `15`, `30` bars

## Best Practical Candidate

The most defensible candidate was not the highest PF row. It was the highest-sample row with both train and holdout PF above `1.3`.

Recommended variant:

- Chart timeframe: `1-minute`
- Direction: long only
- Entry window: `13:30` to before `15:30` ET
- Trend filter: C3 close above EMA20 and session VWAP
- Range filter: C3 range >= 20-bar average range
- Stop: C2 low
- Target: `1.5R`
- Max hold: `10` bars

Result:

## 2-Minute Constraint

If the timeframe must remain `2m`, the setup does not cleanly reach `1.3 PF` with a strong sample size.

Best 2m rows with at least `100` trades stayed below 1.3:

| Setup | Trades | PF |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Afternoon VWAP-trend long, `1R`, 15 bars | `129` | `1.2963` |
| Afternoon EMA20+VWAP long, `1R`, 15 bars | `124` | `1.2836` |
| Afternoon EMA20+VWAP+range all, `2R`, 15 bars | `185` | `1.2816` |

There were 2m rows above `1.3`, but they were smaller samples, mostly `50-95` trades. Treat those as exploratory, not decision-grade.

## Trade Frequency Constraint

The `1.3 PF` target and "scalp daily on SPY only" target conflict.

Using roughly `1,260` trading days in the five-year sample:

| Minimum Trades | Approx Trades/Day | Best PF Found | Readout |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `500` | `0.40/day` | `1.3284` | Still less than one trade every two days |
| `750` | `0.60/day` | `1.1622` | More frequent, but PF no longer acceptable |
| `1,000` | `0.79/day` | `1.0172` | Near breakeven before real option friction |
| `1,500` | `1.19/day` | `0.9941` | Daily frequency, no edge |

Best high-frequency row with at least `1,000` trades:

- Timeframe: `1m`
- Window: `09:35-15:30`
- Filter: VWAP trend
- Side: both
- Target: `1.5R`
- Max hold: `10` bars
- Trades: `1,129`
- PF: `1.0172`

This is not tradable after spreads, slippage, and option execution friction.

Conclusion: SPY-only daily frequency is not available from this 3-bar N pattern while preserving a `1.3` PF threshold. To get daily trade count, the strategy needs one of these changes:

1. expand the universe beyond SPY;
2. use the N pattern as a regime/context filter and add a separate high-frequency entry trigger;
3. accept a lower PF target and prove the options execution model compensates, which is unlikely;
4. use multiple independent setup families rather than forcing one pattern to trade every day.

## Higher-PF But Lower-Sample Alternative

The highest-PF practical row was a 5-minute short-side variant:

- Chart timeframe: `5-minute`
- Direction: short only
- Entry window: `09:35` to before `15:30` ET
- Filter: C3 close below EMA20 and session VWAP
- Range filter: C3 range >= 20-bar average range
- Stop: C2 high
- Target: `1R`
- Max hold: `5` bars

Result:

| Period | Trades | Win Rate | Avg Points | Avg R | PF |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Full sample | `94` | `54.26%` | `0.2459` | `0.1719` | `2.0167` |
| 2021-2024 train | `68` | `50.00%` | `0.0817` | `0.1081` | `1.3403` |
| 2025-2026 holdout | `26` | `65.38%` | `0.6752` | `0.3388` | `3.7408` |

Caveat: only `94` trades. It meets the PF objective, but the sample is thin and year stability is uneven.

## Recommendation

For SPY options work, use the `1m afternoon long-only` variant as the primary candidate because it has the better sample size and passes the simple train/holdout check.

Do not treat this as options-ready yet. The next required step is to map the signal timestamps into an options model using realistic entry/exit assumptions:

- 1-3 DTE or 3-7 DTE calls
- no 0DTE until the underlying edge survives slippage
- enter at ask or midpoint plus slippage
- exit at bid or midpoint minus slippage
- reject contracts with wide spreads
