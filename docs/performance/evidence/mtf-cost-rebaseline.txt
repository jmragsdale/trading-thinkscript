## Question

0AA proved the incumbent package's headline is a B5-lookahead artifact and that the package is a
REJECT on a clean engine. Two jobs remained (BACKLOG 0AC): **(a)** stamp every leak-derived
artifact so no future session re-quotes a void number, and **(b)** re-baseline the farm's
*economics* — monthly income and the multi-firm scorecard — off clean lists, so the project has
real numbers instead of merely a warning that the old ones are fake.

Concretely: what does the farm actually earn, and what does an eval slot actually produce, once
the leak is removed?

## Method

**(b) Re-baseline.** The new script imports 0AA's rescore module rather than re-implementing it,
so trade generation (`clean_trades`), mix construction (`build_mix`) and the combined package
rules (`daily_pnl_with_package_rules` — 5 entries/day, −2.5R/+4.5R lockouts, 1R=$200, combined
across instruments) are byte-identical to what produced the 43.3%/54.4% headline. Only the
downstream reporting is new: monthly aggregation, and a firm-shape campaign sweep.

Two cost conventions are carried side by side throughout, never mixed:
- `incumbent` — fixed per-symbol `COST_R` (MNQ 0.060R / MYM 0.149R / MGC 0.068R) at 1R=$200.
  This is the convention the *old artifacts used*, so it is the apples-to-apples comparison.
- `honest_2.50ct` — engine_v3's per-trade $2.50/contract round turn on actual filled quantity.

Firm-shape sweep: rolling starts, no timeout, closed daily PnL, EOD trailing max loss,
consistency evaluated at pass time, min-trading-days counted on non-zero days — the conventions
of `analysis/mtf_ote_multi_firm_scaling.md`, so the old and new tables are directly comparable.

**Harness validity.** The re-baseline reproduces 0AA exactly on the overlapping cells:
MNQ+MYM/MFFU/incumbent gives P(pass) **0.456**, ≤21d **0.316**, funded/slot **0.623**, net PnL
**$43,301.60** — matching `clean_rescore_scorecard.csv`'s `clean:MNQ+MYM` row to the digit
(0.4561 / 0.3159 / 0.6228 / 43301.60). The honest-cost cell reproduces **−$104,691.63**
identically. So the new numbers below are new *reporting*, not a new model.

**(a) Restamp.** Banner citing 0AA on every artifact named in 0AC, plus two additions found this
session: `reports/mtf_ote_live_ready_report.md` (the source of the "LIVE_READY" verdict itself,
not in the 0AC list) and the six scripts that *read* the leaked CSVs — stamping the outputs alone
leaves the generators live, which is how a future session would re-mint void numbers.

## Verdict

**DONE (remediation) — and the re-baseline CONFIRMS REJECT with the economics now quantified.**

The clean farm is not a smaller version of the published one; it is a different business. At the
old cost convention it earns **$710/mo with 24 of 61 months negative** (published: $4,741 with
zero negative months) and has **lost money over the trailing year**. At honest fills it loses
money in every year of the sample. An eval slot mints **0.62 funded accounts/month at $219 each**
rather than 1.40 at $106 — but that number is close to meaningless, because a 54% fail rate means
the funded accounts produced would be funding a negative-expectancy strategy.

Nothing here changes the standing conclusion from 0AA: **the farm has no validated live
strategy.** What changes is that the project's economic claims are now quoted from something real
instead of from a warning label.
